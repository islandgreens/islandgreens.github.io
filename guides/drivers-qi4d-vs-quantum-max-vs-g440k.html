Qi4D vs. Quantum Max vs. G440 K

Three flagship drivers, three different bets on where speed and forgiveness should come from. Includes verified tour usage, checked against multiple independent sources before a single name went on this page.

TaylorMade's Qi4D replaced the Qi35 in January 2026 after a rough year of manufacturing consistency complaints. Callaway's Quantum Max introduced a face material combination the industry hadn't used before. Ping's G440 K pushed the brand's own MOI record higher than any adjustable driver it's built. All three launched within days of each other at the start of 2026, and all three are legitimate flagship options, not a budget pick among them.

Qi4D's list is the deepest of the three, and it's backed by matching detail across TaylorMade's own press materials, PGA Tour's official equipment reporting, and independent WITB breakdowns from Golf Monthly, MyGolfSpy, and GolfWRX, all naming the same players with the same specific head and loft. Nelly Korda's move is especially well documented: multiple outlets tracked her switch away from the Qi10 Max at the start of the season and confirmed it again after her Chevron Championship win.

Quantum's tour list leans on PGA Tour's own equipment desk, which quoted Callaway directly confirming Schauffele, Bhatia, Si Woo Kim, and Greyserman by name, plus separate on-site reporting from Golf Monthly and Today's Golfer at the Dubai Invitational and Sony Open. Rose Zhang's usage comes from a single but specific sighting; it's included because it's a named, dated report, not a vague "spotted" credit, but it's worth flagging as thinner than the men's list.

The G440 K story is the most interesting one to get right, precisely because it's not a clean marketing win. Viktor Hovland genuinely switched into it, confirmed by GOLF's on-site tour equipment desk at TPC Scottsdale. Ben Griffin tested the G440 K extensively that same week, by some accounts had it fully dialed in, and then chose to stay with his older G430 Max 10K anyway. A site that only reports the switches and quietly ignores the players who tried a club and passed on it isn't giving you the real picture. Both facts are true, and only one of them is a sale.

Island Greens Pick

Take Qi4D if you want the most balanced, best-tested-overall driver of the three; independent testing has it finishing first in more than one major 2026 driver test, and the tour list backs that up on both tours. Take Quantum Max if face innovation and price matter to you, it's the only one of the three built on a titanium, poly mesh, and carbon fiber face, and it's $50 to $100 cheaper than the other two. Take G440 K if forgiveness is the entire point, nothing here is built for straighter, more consistent misses the way this one is, and the CG-shifting weight is a genuine upgrade over its predecessor rather than a cosmetic tweak.
